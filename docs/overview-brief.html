Handshake v4 · live since 19 November 2026

Peer-to-peer OTC that never holds your funds

Handshake lets anyone swap BDAG for any ERC-20 token on BlockDAG (chain 1404), or the reverse, at a price they set, with no order book, no pooled liquidity and no middleman. Both sides settle in one transaction, or nothing moves.

What changed in v4: earlier versions were an escrow: the maker's BDAG or tokens sat in the contract until a fill or a cancel. v4 stores only the terms of an offer. The maker's side stays in their own wallet under an exact approval until someone fills it, and the fill moves both sides wallet to wallet. The contract holds nothing, ever: not between trades, not during them.

How a trade works

1. ApproveThe maker approves exactly the amount they're offering. Selling BDAG? It's wrapped 1:1 to WBDAG (Reef's wrapped BDAG) first, in one click.
2. PostThe maker names the price, and can make the offer private to one address and give it an expiry date.
3. FillThe taker pays; both sides move in one transaction. A BDAG seller's WBDAG is unwrapped so the taker gets real BDAG.
4. Or cancelAny time. There's nothing to refund because nothing left the wallet. Revoking the approval also stops fills.

If a maker spends or moves their funds after posting, the fill simply fails and the taker keeps everything; the site greys the offer out as "not fillable" before anyone tries.
