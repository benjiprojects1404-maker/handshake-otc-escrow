# Handshake v4.1

`Handshake_OTC_Escrow/contracts/HandshakeV4_1.sol`: Handshake v4 with the fixes Chord OTC v2.1 got after its
Olympix pre-audit scan and Proovant's report. Chord v2 and Handshake v4 share the same non-custodial design, so the
same findings applied here. v4 (`HandshakeV4.sol`) is kept unchanged as the record of what's deployed.

Not independently audited.

## What changes

| Finding (on Chord) | Handshake v4.1 |
|---|---|
| Olympix 5.1.1, Proovant CHORD-01: an approval given for a new offer could bring an older offer that lost its backing back to life at its old price | `committed[maker][asset]` is the total promised by a maker's open offers (WBDAG when they give BDAG). A new offer needs balance and approval for that total plus the new amount; fill and cancel release it. An expired offer stays committed until cancelled. |
| Olympix 5.2.1: a fee change could land before the maker's create | `createOffer` takes a last argument `uint16 maxFeeBps` and reverts with "fee above your max" if the fee is higher. `OfferCreated` gains a final `uint16 feeBps`. |
| Proovant CHORD-02: one approval backs both your own offers and your fills | When the taker pays with a token, the fill reverts if it would leave the taker's own open offers on that token short of balance or approval. Paying in BDAG touches nothing committed. |
| Pre-audit F-01 | Token moves are checked at both ends: the payer loses, and the receiver gains, exactly the amount. |
| Pre-audit F-05, F-21/F-22 | The fee recipient can't be the contract; `setPaused(bool newPaused)`. |
| Olympix 5.2.2 (docs) | `maxOfferAmount` limits both legs of an offer (it already did; comments say so now). |
| (Chord v2 already had it) | Two-step ownership: `transferOwnership` names a pending owner, who calls `acceptOwnership()`. |

ABI changes for the site and anything else that creates offers: the extra `maxFeeBps` argument, the extra
`feeBps` event field, and the new `committed(address,address)` view. Fill is unchanged.

## Tests

`forge test` (Solidity 0.8.24, EVM berlin): 74 tests pass, including
`test/foundry/HandshakeV4_1.t.sol` (all of v4's tests, ported) and `test/foundry/HandshakeV4_1.fixes.t.sol` (18 new
tests: the stale-offer, max-fee and taker-backing cases, fuzz tests, F-01/F-05, and an invariant that `committed`
always equals the sum of each maker's open offers). Removing each fix makes its tests fail.

## Deploy (Remix, then the multisig)

1. Remix: Solidity **0.8.24**, optimizer on, 200 runs, EVM **berlin** (BlockDAG has no PUSH0). Or load
   `remix-v4_1/HandshakeV4_1_standard_input.json`, which is also the input for explorer verification. About 9.6 KB
   of runtime bytecode.
2. Deploy `HandshakeV4_1` with the same values as v4:
   - `wbdag`: `0x62ba5c4F067989a7f6644488C875bEa69Bfa1FBA` (Reef's WBDAG)
   - `_feeRecipient`: `0x26bDba7b184df8b88965Bed46AEd6ea0E60fF940`
3. From the deployer, before handing over: `setMaxOfferAmount(0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000,
   1000000000000000000000000)` (1M BDAG, same as v4) and any token caps v4 has.
4. `transferOwnership(0x4E2401bFD24c66166fABF9Cc5cD5B6B2c5c860fc)` (the 2-of-2 multisig), then from the multisig:
   submit → confirm → execute `acceptOwnership()` on the v4.1 address (calldata `0x79ba5097`). Check the proposal
   shows `executed: true` and `owner()` is the multisig.
5. Smoke test: a small offer each way, filled from a second wallet; check the contract holds nothing.
6. Site: switch the desk to v4.1 (separate PR, once the address is known).

**Don't pause v4 straight away.** Pausing v4 also stops fills of its open offers. On 24 Nov 2026 v4 had one open
offer (#2: 2,500 BDAG for 1 of token `0x3d2661a0…33c4`, no expiry). Point the site at v4.1 first, keep v4's offers
fillable and cancellable from a v4 page, and pause v4 from the multisig once it has none open.
